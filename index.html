Hello, I’m Trevor. I build human-centered products for companies and organizations amidst periods of transformation.

Nine to five, I lead product strategy at IA Collaborative, an innovation consultancy designing solutions to industry leaders’ most complex challenges. Two recent projects: an AI Workbench for a Fortune 100 pharma company’s brand teams, and a digital front-door for international patients at the world’s leading cancer center.

I also co-founded General Purpose, a technology studio using frontier intelligence to shape our collective future. We build tools like Glottomap, which crowd-sources benchmarks for low-resource languages, and Canopy, a no-code machine-learning pipeline for tagging and classification of camera-trap imagery.

In the past, I served as an entrepreneur-in-residence at a blockchain venture studio, a product manager at a healthcare startup, and a founder of an activist brand in the outdoor industry. I studied design engineering at Harvard, and cultural anthropology at Colorado College.

Home is Jackson, Wyoming. I like to sit in silence at meditation retreats every couple of years, and float on rivers (I’ve rafted through the Grand Canyon nine times). I live for wild places and inspired people.
